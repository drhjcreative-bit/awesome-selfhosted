Volume One

Quiet the Critic

A workbook for silencing self-doubt & reclaiming your worth

The critic in your head didn't come from nowhere — it came from somewhere. This workbook helps you trace it to its source, talk back to it with the voice you'd use for someone you love, and rebuild from the inside. Not by earning your worth, but by remembering you already have it.

  • 01Know Your Critic — where it came from, what it says, what it costs you
  • 02Talk Back — compassion as practice, evidence over feelings
  • 03Honor Your Worth — why worth isn't earned, the quieter voice

Volume Six

Built, Not Born

The art & science of getting happier

You're not unhappy — you're deficient in one of three things. Built on the happiness research of social scientist Arthur C. Brooks, this workbook teaches the three macronutrients of a nourishing life — enjoy, achieve, serve — then has you count what's actually on your plate before you decide what your problem is.

  • 01The Wrong Target — why feeling better was never the goal
  • 02The Three Plates — enjoy, achieve, serve — taught, then counted
  • 03Where They Get Built — family, friendship, work, and the fourth thing
80 guided pages Self-scales & counts Enjoy · Achieve · Serve
